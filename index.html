About

Ohkola Industrial Security Oy is an independent OT/IT cybersecurity and industrial network engineering practice based in Mäntsälä, Finland.

28+ years of network and security engineering experience, focused on industrial control systems: the network architecture, hardware, and segmentation that keep operational technology running independently of the enterprise IT environment around it. Work spans offshore platforms, energy infrastructure, manufacturing and factory sites, and utilities including water.

Work is taken as single contracts, one engagement at a time — full attention on one client's network rather than a portfolio spread thin.
